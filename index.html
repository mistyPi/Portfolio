About

Always on that computer...

Ever since the rise of the internet, I knew I wanted to be inside on the computer. What started as a hobby — building and coding video games, customizing webpages, and generally figuring out how things worked — eventually turned into a pursuit of a career.

Now, two diplomas later, a handful of active clients, and a habit of caring about the small details more than anyone asked me to, I've turned that curiosity into a well-rounded set of skills across web development, design, branding, and digital marketing. Alongside my client work, I work as a Centre Director at Code Ninjas, where I get to combine my technical background with leadership, communication, and teaching.

I like taking an idea from a blank page and turning it into something that looks good, works well, and serves a purpose. Whether it's building a website, developing a brand, or helping a client get their work in front of the right people, I enjoy bringing all the pieces together.
